LexiMind inglizcha lug‘ati — manbalar va litsenziyalar

LexiMind combines English WordNet synsets with Uzbek lemmas by WordNet 3.0 synset ID. Headwords are normalized, and lesson entries are selected by CEFR level and part of speech.

English entries and glosses: Princeton WordNet 3.0, https://wordnet.princeton.edu/
Uzbek lemmas: UzWordnet 1.0 (Agostini, Usmanov, Khamdamov, Abdurakhmonova, and Mamasaidov, 2021), https://github.com/LDKR-Group/UzWordnet and https://uzwordnet.ldkr.org/. CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.
A1–B2 classifications: The CEFR-J Wordlist Version 1.5, compiled by Yukio Tono, Tokyo University of Foreign Studies. Tono Laboratory ©. https://www.cefr-j.org/download.html and https://github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj, accessed 25 November 2026.
C1–C2 classifications: Octanove Vocabulary Profile C1/C2 1.0, Octanove Labs, https://github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj. CC BY-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.
